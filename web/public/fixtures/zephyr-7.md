# Zephyr-7 pilot plant — operations memo

Summary: The Zephyr-7 pilot plant produced 41.7 MWh on 3 March 2026. Output was 11% above the forecast of 37.6 MWh because the
second heat exchanger (HX-2) ran for the full 24 hours for the first time since commissioning. Downtime: 0 minutes. Site lead: R. Okafor.
Next milestone: 50 MWh/day sustained by June 2026.
